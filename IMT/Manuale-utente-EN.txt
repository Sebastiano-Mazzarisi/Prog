MENU IMT - USER GUIDE
The IMT Lucca canteen menu on your phone and computer

Address: https://sebastiano-mazzarisi.github.io/Prog/IMT/Menu-IMT.html
Period covered: autumn menu, from September 21 to December 20, 2026,
lunch and dinner every day.


1. WHAT IT IS

Menu IMT shows what is served today at the IMT canteen, lunch and dinner,
in Italian or English. It opens in any browser, there is nothing to sign
up for and it is free. You can save it to your phone's Home screen and
use it like an app.


2. HOW TO OPEN IT

- Tap the link you received, or scan the QR code on the poster in the
  canteen with your phone camera.
- The right meal is shown straight away: until 3 pm today's lunch, after
  3 pm today's dinner. If there is no menu today, the next available day
  is shown.


3. WHAT YOU SEE

At the top is the title box, blue for lunch and yellow for dinner:
- "IMT - LUNCH" or "IMT - DINNER", with a speaker next to it;
- the date, for example "Saturday November 28, 2026";
- on the sides, the arrows to go to the previous or next meal.

Below the title is the counter row:
- the Italian flag on the left, the British flag on the right;
- in the middle, in a light blue oval, "2 users online now out of 12".

Then the four courses: First Courses, Main Courses, Side Dishes, Fruit &
Dessert.
- IN BOLD CAPITALS are the dishes of the day, which change every day.
- In regular type are the fixed alternatives, always available (for
  example plain pasta, mixed salad).

At the bottom of the page:
- on the left, "by Mazzarisi": tap it to open the notes, with allergen
  information in the canteen's original PDFs, links to those PDFs and
  to the full Excel file, and the credits for the voices;
- on the right, the PDF oval, the speaker for this guide and the share
  icon.


4. CHANGING MEAL

- Tap the right arrow for the next meal and the left arrow for the
  previous one. The order is lunch, dinner, next day's lunch, and so on.
- On a phone you can also swipe: from right to left to go forward, from
  left to right to go back.
- On a computer you can use the left and right arrow keys.
- To refresh the page, pull down when you are at the top. You stay on
  the same meal and in the same language.


5. LANGUAGE: ITALIAN OR ENGLISH

- Tap the Italian or the British flag. The one in use is full, the other
  is faded.
- You can also switch language by tapping anywhere on the menu.
- The page remembers your choice and opens in the same language next
  time.


6. LISTENING TO THE MENU

- Tap the title (or the speaker next to it) to hear the menu read aloud.
  Tap it again to stop.
- In Italian the reader is Serena, in English Cori: natural, free voices.
- The reading gives a greeting with the date, the name of each course and
  the dishes of the day, joined by "or", then a closing greeting. For
  example: "First courses. Pea soup or pasta with meat sauce."
- The fixed alternatives are not read, to keep it short.
- If the audio is not available, the phone's or computer's own voice is
  used.


7. HOW MANY PEOPLE USE THE MENU

- The oval below the title shows how many people have the page open
  right now and how many devices have used it in total. It updates every
  5 seconds.
- Each device is recognised by an anonymous code: no names, numbers or
  other personal data are collected.
- Tap the oval to open the list of devices:
  - at the top the total, "Registered devices: 12";
  - for each one, the day and time of first use;
  - in red the devices online now, in blue the others; in each group the
    most recent is at the top;
  - on the right, the language used: IT in blue, EN in green.
- Your own device is one of the red ones: tap its row to give it a name
  (up to 30 characters), for example "Anna's phone". Confirm with OK or
  Enter.
- Close the list with the X or by tapping outside it.


8. MENU AS A PDF: READ AND PRINT

- At the bottom of the page, tap the PDF oval. The PDF file of the meal
  you are looking at opens, a portrait A4 sheet. From there, with the
  Share button of your phone or browser, you can send it, save it (on
  iPhone: Save to Files) or print it.
- The sheet is always in Italian and English:
  - at the top, title and date in both languages, for example
    "IMT - PRANZO | LUNCH";
  - for each course a table with Italian on the left and English on the
    right, line by line;
  - at the bottom, the QR code of the page, handy for anyone who finds
    the printed sheet.
- Without a connection the sheet appears inside the page, with two
  buttons at the top:
  - "Print / Save PDF" opens your device's print dialog; from there
    choose a printer or "Save as PDF" (on iPhone: Print, then Share and
    Save to Files);
  - "Exit" goes back to the menu; on a computer the Esc key works too.
- The menu always fits on a single A4 page.


9. LISTENING TO THIS GUIDE

- At the bottom of the page, just to the right of the PDF oval, there is
  a speaker. Tap it to hear this guide read aloud: in Italian by Serena
  if the page is in Italian, in English by Cori if it is in English.
- While it plays, the speaker is highlighted. Tap it again to pause; tap
  it once more to resume from where it stopped.


10. SHARING AND SAVING TO THE HOME SCREEN

Tap the share icon (the small square with an arrow pointing up), at the
bottom right. A panel opens with:
- Share the link: send it by WhatsApp, email, messages...
- Copy the link: paste it wherever you like.
- Add to Home screen: instructions for your device.

In short, to have it on the Home screen like an app:
- iPhone (Safari): Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
- Android (Chrome): the three-dot menu, then "Add to Home screen" or
  "Install app".
- Windows PC: in Edge, menu "...", then Apps, then "Install this site as
  an app"; in Chrome, the three-dot menu, then "Cast, save and share",
  then "Install page as app".
- Mac: in Safari, File menu, then "Add to Dock".

Installed this way, the menu opens with one tap from its icon and also
works on a weak connection.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The menu does not update, I still see the old one?
  Pull the page down when you are at the top, or close it and open it
  again.

I can't hear the voice.
  Check that the volume is up and the phone is not in silent mode. On
  iPhone the side silent switch also mutes the sound of web pages.

Where can I find allergen information?
  Tap "by Mazzarisi" at the bottom of the page: it has links to the
  canteen's original menu PDFs, with the allergen information.

Am I counted more than once?
  No: each device is counted only once, however many times it opens the
  page. A person's phone and computer, though, count as two devices.

Do I need to sign up or pay?
  No. The menu is open and free.
